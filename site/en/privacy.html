Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Data controller

Ruggiero Russo – ERRE studio, Barletta (BT), Italy · VAT 07989340729 · Email: info@ruggierorusso.com.

What data we collect

This website is a photography portfolio with no sign-up. Browsing data: like any website, the hosting server automatically logs technical data (IP address, browser type, date and time of the request, page visited). It is used only to run and secure the site, never for profiling. Email: if you write to info@ruggierorusso.com, your address and message are used only to reply and handle your request. Studio booking form: if you fill it in we collect your name, email, optional company and phone and the project details you enter. Requests are received through the Netlify Forms service and forwarded to our email; we use them only to reply and confirm the booking.

Cookies and analytics

This website uses no cookies and no analytics, advertising or tracking tools. Fonts, images and scripts are hosted on the site itself: no third-party services are loaded while you browse, so no cookie banner is needed. Moodboard: if you save photos to the moodboard, the list stays only in your browser's local storage and is not sent to us unless you choose to email it to us. You can delete it at any time with the «Clear» button.

Legal basis

Browsing data: legitimate interest in running and securing the site (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Email: replying to your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Providers and transfers

The site is hosted by Netlify, Inc. (United States), which processes browsing data as a processor. Domain and email are managed by Aruba S.p.A. (Italy). Any transfer of data outside the European Union relies on the safeguards provided by the GDPR (adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses).

Retention

Browsing data is kept by the hosting provider only as long as needed for the security of the service. Emails are kept as long as needed to handle the request and to meet legal obligations.

Your rights

You can ask at any time for access, rectification, erasure, restriction of or objection to processing, and data portability (Arts. 15–22 GDPR) by writing to info@ruggierorusso.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (garanteprivacy.it).

Data deletion

If you wish to request deletion of data about you, email info@ruggierorusso.com with the subject "Data deletion": you will receive confirmation within 30 days.
